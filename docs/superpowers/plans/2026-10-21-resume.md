# Resume, Continue watching and Up next (piece 2 of 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Playing a NAS or local disc saves a "place" (disc, playlist, time, tracks, text subtitles, shader, volume, registers) per person on the NAS (per device for local discs); opening that disc again offers Resume or Start from beginning; the title library shows a Continue watching row whose finished series episodes become Up next.

**Architecture:** The engine gains two things: an `open` that starts at a given playlist and time (directly, or by resuming an HDMV title with its saved registers), and a `readPlace` command answered by a `placeState` event with the registers. The UI builds a place from the controller snapshot plus that event (`place-model.ts`, pure), and `PlaceSaver` writes it to an IndexedDB outbox, then to the NAS (`/api/me/playback/…`, `PlaybackStore` under `/data/users/<personId>/playback/`). The NAS works out Continue watching and Up next from the live catalogue (`up-next.ts`, pure). Play links switch to the engine's start-at-open, which removes piece 1's First Play race.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (strict), React 19, Vitest, Playwright, the NAS server on plain Node.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-permalinks-resume-design.md` §6 (this plan) and §5 (links, built). §7 (subtitle store) is piece 3: no stored-subtitle fields here.

## Global Constraints

- A place is per person, per disc **and playlist** (`discKey` + playlist id); the disc's settings (tracks, text subtitles and offsets, shader, volume) are also kept per disc as defaults for its other playlists (spec §6.1).
- Which playback makes a place: a playlist the catalogue knows as an episode or the disc's main title, or any playlist of 5 minutes or more; shorter ones never create or overwrite a place; stopping while a menu shows keeps the last qualifying place (spec §6.2). Constant `PLACE_MIN_DURATION_US = 300_000_000`; test-only `?placemin=<seconds>` overrides it (listed with the other test flags in CLAUDE.md).
- Saved every 10 s while playing, and at once on pause, seek, track change, offset change, playlist change, `visibilitychange` to hidden and `pagehide`; on `pagehide` over HTTPS with `fetch(…, { keepalive: true })`; each save goes to IndexedDB first and is sent from there (spec §6.2).
- Player-setting PSRs 13, 15–21, 23–31, 48–61 are saved but never restored (spec §6.1).
- HDMV resume: write the saved GPRs and PSRs, start title PSR4, open the playlist at the saved item and time; if the title won't start there, play the playlist directly and log it (spec §6.3). BD-J discs: play the playlist directly at the saved time with the navigator and BD-J off; Menu boots BD-J fresh; only PSRs 1–3 are restored, no GPRs (spec §6.3).
- Finished: `ended`, or past the start of the last chapter when that chapter starts in the final 10% of the playlist, or past 95% with no chapters (spec §6.5). A finished place drops off Continue watching and opens from the beginning without a prompt.
- Up next: next episode by number in the same season, then the next season's first episode, on whichever disc; none known, or the last episode → the series drops off; the card reads "Up next · S1 · Episode 4", no progress bar, opens from the beginning with no prompt; it carries audio and subtitle choices by language, plus shader and volume; movies and music have no Up next (spec §6.6).
- NAS store: `/data/users/<personId>/playback/<discKey>.json`, atomic writes (`server/src/atomic-json.ts`), routes for the token's own person only; `GET /api/me/playback` lists unfinished places newest first, capped at 50; at most 500 places per person, oldest finished pruned first (spec §6.4). Never touch `/data/users/<personId>/bdj/` (BD-J 5e's).
- Local discs: IndexedDB keyed by the browser-computed fingerprint (`discKeyOf`); not in the NAS's Continue watching; offered on reopen and on Recent discs (spec §6.4).
- Resume prompt copy (mockup 2): heading "Pick up where you left off?", line "Stopped at <m:ss> of <m:ss> on <deviceName>, <when>." plus the audio, subtitle (with offset) and shader in use, buttons **Resume at <m:ss>** (primary) and **Start from beginning**. Continue watching card: title, "S<n> · Episode <m> · <k> min left" or "Movie · <h> h <mm> min left", progress bar; Up next card: "Up next · S<n> · Episode <m>", no bar.
- `src/engine/` never imports `src/ui/`; the NAS server has no native dependencies and writes only `/data`; the repo is public (made-up names in tests).
- Run the NAS E2E by full path with `E2E_PORT=<free port>`; ports 4191/4192 and UDP 47990 are shared with other checkouts.

## Review Focus

1. **Two devices saving the same place** (iPad and iPhone): the newer `updatedAt` wins, and a stale outbox entry sent later never overwrites a newer server place. Pinned in Tasks 4 and 7.
2. **The disc changed since the place was saved** (playlist gone, time past its end): the prompt says so and offers only Start from beginning; nothing seeks blindly. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 8.
3. **The NAS unreachable while watching** (offline, WebRTC dropped): playback never blocks or errors; saves queue on the device and go out on the next connection. Pinned in Task 7.
4. **A short menu or trailer playlist after a long one** (HDMV menus loop backgrounds): the long playlist's place survives and stays the one offered. Pinned in Task 6.
5. **A guest and an owner on one NAS**: each sees only their own places and Continue watching; another person's `discKey` place is never readable. Pinned in Task 4.
